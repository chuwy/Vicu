# Vicu 2.3.0

Vicu 2.3.0 ships together with Vicu 2.3.0 for Android and iOS. You can now add tasks by voice, and give a task a reminder for when you arrive at or leave a place your phone saved. New tasks with a due date also get your default reminder. Update your phones to 2.3.0 at the same time.

The phone side is in the [Vicu 2.3.0 for Android and iOS notes](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/releases/tag/v2.3.0). If you find a rough edge, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/issues).

### Add tasks by voice

- **Hold + to say a task.** Hold the + (in the header, the New task row or an empty list) and speak: the words show in a card as they are recognized, with the date, project and labels picked out the way quick add reads them. Let go to add the task, press Esc or slide away to cancel. A click still opens the composer.
- The task goes to the list on screen: a project adds to that project, Today gives today's date, a custom list uses its add-to project. A project or date you say wins.
- **A voice shortcut** can do the same without the mouse: record one in Settings, Keyboard Shortcuts (it is off until you do). Hold it to talk, or tap it to keep listening until you press it again or Enter. "Say a task" in the command palette starts it too.
- Speech runs on macOS (on the device when your language allows it) and Windows. Vicu asks for microphone and speech access the first time. On Linux the composer opens instead.

### Location reminders

- **Remind me at a place**: the reminder popover has an "At a place" section with the places your phones saved (Home, Work, ...): When I arrive or When I leave, within 100, 200 or 500 m. Your phone reminds you there. The chip reads "Arrive: Home".
- **Type it**: "Call mom when I get home" in the composer or Quick Entry sets the reminder for a saved place, with a chip that says so.
- Places are set up on a phone; Settings, Notifications lists them. Where a place is never leaves the phone.

### More

- **Default reminder**: Settings, Notifications, Default reminder now applies to every new task with a due date: in the composer, Quick Entry and pasted lines. A reminder you pick yourself wins, and changing the date of an existing task never adds one.
- **Save an attachment**: each attachment has a Save button next to Open, which starts in Downloads.
- **Routines have one type**: a name, times (each with Remind me and Follow up) and a schedule, each explained. A missed day is "Not logged"; routines are never overdue. Older versions keep reading them.
- The open task's "Checklist" chip is now "Subtasks".

### Fixes

- Opening or saving an attachment that fails now says so instead of doing nothing.
