# Vicu 2.5.1

Vicu 2.5.1 helps you deal with the tasks you have put off: guided sessions walk you through them one decision at a time. It also lets you hide the smart lists you do not use, and subtasks can now be renamed, opened and deleted from an open task.

If you find a rough edge, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/issues).

### Guided organizing

- **One task at a time.** Vicu picks what needs a decision and shows one card at a time with how far along you are. Give a task a date, move it to another project, change its title, complete it, delete it or skip it, then go on with Next. At the end a summary says what changed, with **Undo all**.
- **Four sessions:** Sort the Inbox, Sort out overdue, Waiting a long time (tasks nobody touched for 60 days or more) and Review projects. Start them from the Inbox header, the Review view, the command palette or Ask.
- **Project review** shows each project due for review with a health line, its untouched tasks and its next step. Keep or archive it, then mark it reviewed.
- **Weekly review** (in Review or the command palette): a checklist that takes you through the Inbox, a brain dump, the past week, overdue tasks, the next two weeks, your projects and what has waited too long. Finish records the review and syncs it to your phone.
- **Works without the assistant.** With Ask set up, the model suggests a decision for each card and says why; you still decide.
- The keyboard does everything: Enter for Next, Left for Back, S to skip, 1 to 4 for quick dates, Esc to leave.

### Sidebar

- **Hide the smart lists you do not use.** Settings, General, Sidebar has a checkbox for Today, Upcoming, Anytime and Logbook, and for Routines and Review while they are on. The Inbox always stays. A hidden list still works from the command palette. This is set per computer.

### Subtasks in an open task

- **Rename a subtask** by clicking its title. Enter saves, Escape cancels.
- **Open** and **Delete** buttons show when you point at a subtask, and right-click offers Rename, Open subtask and Delete subtask.
- **Delete** follows "Confirm before deleting" and, when the subtask has subtasks of its own, asks whether to delete them too.
- **Open subtask** opens it as its own card, right under its parent.
- A renamed or deleted subtask now changes in the card straight away.
