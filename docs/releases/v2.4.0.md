# Vicu 2.4.0

Vicu 2.4.0 ships together with Vicu 2.4.0 for Android and iOS. You can now share a routine with someone you already share a project with, and Vicu keeps syncing on a server whose search is broken. Update your phones to 2.4.0 at the same time.

The phone side is in the [Vicu 2.4.0 for Android and iOS notes](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/releases/tag/v2.4.0). If you find a rough edge, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/issues).

### Share a routine

- **Share with**, in the routine editor, lists the people of the projects you already share. Pick one and the routine moves into that project; Vicu never creates or shares a project for you. People who can only view a project are listed as "Can view only". When the person is in more than one of your projects, choose which one keeps the routine. "Only you" stops sharing.
- A tick or skip by either of you shows for both, and a finished time says who did it ("Done by Maya"). Two people ticking at the same moment both count.
- Members who can edit the project tick and edit the routine; view-only members only look. Only you, the owner, can stop sharing, archive or delete it.
- **Your own choices**: as a member, turn the routine's reminders off for you, or hide it. Hidden routines are listed in Settings, Routines, where you can show them again. Both follow you to your other devices.
- A shared routine's reminder looks at the routine again when it is due, so a time someone else already ticked stays quiet.
- Deleting or archiving a project that holds a shared routine says so first, even with delete confirmations off.

### Fixes

- **Syncing with a server whose search is broken.** A Vikunja server on ParadeDB whose `pg_search` extension is older than its library (a newer image pulled without running `ALTER EXTENSION pg_search UPDATE`) answers every search with "unexpected error occurred". Custom lists, synced settings and routines then failed with that error whenever you had none yet, and older routine history did not load. They load now; searching in Vicu works again once the server is fixed. ([Vicu-Mobile#51](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/issues/51))
