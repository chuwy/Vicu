# Vicu 2.5.0

Vicu 2.5.0 ships together with Vicu 2.5.0 for Android and iOS. It adds an assistant that runs on the AI model you choose: talk and your tasks appear as cards, organize a long brain dump into projects and tasks, and ask about your tasks by typing or by voice. Speech is recognized on your computer, and projects can be grouped under categories. Update your phones to 2.5.0 at the same time.

The phone side is in the [Vicu 2.5.0 for Android and iOS notes](https://github.com/rendyhd/Vicu-Mobile/releases/tag/v2.5.0). If you find a rough edge, please [open an issue](https://github.com/rendyhd/vicu/issues).

### An assistant with your own model

- **Off until you set it up**, in Settings, Assistant. Choose a provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Ollama, Open WebUI or any OpenAI-compatible server), paste your key and pick a model; Vicu tests the model as soon as you choose it. There is no Vicu server: requests go from your computer straight to your provider, with your key, which is kept encrypted on this computer. Before anything goes to a cloud provider, Vicu shows what each feature sends and asks you to agree. Each feature in Settings says where to find it and has **Try it**.
- **Quick capture stays as it was.** Hold + or the voice shortcut and let go, and what you said becomes one task, without the model.
- **Smart dictation.** Tap the voice shortcut instead of holding it, slide up to the sparkle from a held +, or choose "Dictate tasks" in the command palette, and keep talking. Each pause turns what you said into task cards with dates, projects, labels, priority, reminders and steps, and Vicu marks what it guessed. Say what to change ("make the first one Friday", "and call mum tomorrow") and the cards follow. Add adds them with one Undo. Also **Make tasks from text** in the command palette, and **Break into steps** when you right-click a task.
- **Brain dump.** Turn on Brain dump in the dictation panel, or choose "Brain dump" in the command palette, and talk or type for as long as you like: nothing is sent until you press **Organize**. Everything is then sorted into tasks with notes, subtasks and priority, in your projects or in **new projects and subprojects** where none of yours fit. Rename or remove a new project, change a card, or say or type a change and press **Update**. **Add** creates the new projects and then the tasks, with one Undo. New projects need a connection.
- **Ask.** The Ask button next to Search, Cmd+J (Ctrl+J on Windows and Linux) or "Ask Vicu" in the command palette. Ask answers about your tasks briefly; changes come as cards that you apply or decline, with Undo. A conversation left for 30 minutes makes way for a new one.
- **Talk.** Turn on the microphone in Ask and just talk: a pause sends it, the answer is read aloud, and it keeps listening. Talk again to interrupt.

### Speech on your computer

- In Settings, Assistant, Hearing, download a speech model and your voice is recognized on this computer, without sending audio anywhere. Whisper large-v3 turbo is the one for computers.

### Project categories

- Optional named headers that group your top-level projects in the sidebar, in Settings, Projects and in the project pickers. Use **Add category above** or **Move to category…** in a project's menu; rename, move or delete a category (with Undo) from its header, or drag a project past a header into that category. Collapsing a category is remembered on this computer.
- Categories sync with your other devices and the phone app. Without any, the sidebar is as before.
